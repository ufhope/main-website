OUR PLATFORM
Join us in our fight to make a better University of Florida: Our main goals are as follows:

Make Campus Affordable

  • Make a 24/7 study space on Campus (Newell Hall).
  • Give a repair budget to RecSports so the gyms can operate without going into debt.
  • Increase funding for print services and add color printing.
  • Work with UF to increase funding for RTS bus routes on and off campus.
  • Work with the Board of Trustees to expand UF’s childcare program (Baby Gator) and work to provide discounted slots for campus employees
  • Fix UF healthcare by ensuring Student Health fees apply at Shands, and work with the SHCC to cover all health expenses.
  • Push against increases to tuition, attendance costs, meal plans, and other student expenses.

A Fair and Transparent Student Government

  • Implement secured online voting for all students.
  • Bring back the multi-district model in elections, and create an independent committee on electoral districts.
  • Remove the "I Voted" stickers from elections, making vote-buying harder.
  • Implement proportional ranked-choice voting (Meek STV) in all elections, ensuring minority voices are represented.
  • Ensure Student Government follows state law on transparency (Sunshine Law).
  • Redesign the Student Government website to make it less confusing and easier to understand.

Protect Immigrant and International Students

  • Tell administration to cut ties with ICE and end UFPD’s 287(g) agreement.
  • Expand Student Legal Services to provide legal counsel for immigration law cases.
  • Create a Secretary for International and Immigrant Students with the goal of helping ensure UF cares better for our students.
  • Fight back against predatory low wages for immigrant students in campus employment.

Go Green

  • Require biodegradable materials to be used at the Reitz Union.
  • Plant more trees across campus and manage them sustainably.
  • Protect green spaces on campus, including Graham Woods, from development.
  • Make campus infrastructure more environmentally friendly while maintaining walkability.
  • Reduce the use of AI-powered systems to track students on campus, including Flock, Axis, and Student Engagement AI.
